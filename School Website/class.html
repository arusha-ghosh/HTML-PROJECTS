Our Classes

Our School offers a wide range of classes to nurture students’ academic and personal growth. Our curriculum is designed to provide strong foundational knowledge while encouraging creativity, critical thinking, and practical skills.

  • Pre-Primary: Nursery, LKG, UKG – Focusing on early learning, social skills, and creative play.
  • Primary: Classes 1 to 5 – Emphasis on literacy, numeracy, and holistic development through arts and activities.
  • Middle School: Classes 6 to 8 – Building strong academic foundations with introduction to science, technology, and life skills.
  • Secondary: Classes 9 and 10 – Preparing students for board exams with in-depth study and practical learning.
  • Higher Secondary: Classes 11 and 12 – Focus on specialized streams and career guidance for future success.

All our classes are conducted by experienced and dedicated teachers who provide individual attention and support. Modern classrooms and laboratories ensure an engaging and interactive learning environment for every student.
